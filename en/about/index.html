About us

One team, one club

Twelve students, seven working groups, three coaches. The name 思无涯 means learning has no shore.

A club that has run since 2018

The Bachuan Quantum robotics club started in 2018 and has competed in robot exploration, firefighting, FLL and FTC. Around a hundred students are members today.

Team 24667 was formed in September 2025 with six Year 10 and six Year 12 students — eight boys, four girls. This season ran from reading the manual to task analysis, structure reviews, code testing and drive practice.

01 / What we are trying to do

Learn to solve problems, then take it outside

01

Build it first

A hundred thoughts are worth less than one rough build. V1 and V2 only failed after they existed — which is exactly when we could start fixing them.

02

Write it down

Same night. Leave it a week and all you remember is “something changed”.

03

Explain it

When a judge asks why we picked this, we have to answer. If we cannot, we have not thought it through.

04

Pass it on

Year 12 leaves the robot and the notes behind. The next lot do not start from zero.
