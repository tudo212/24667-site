Gracious Professionalism

Compete hard, stay decent

Woodie Flowers' idea. We put it in two lines: win without gloating, lose without excuses. If a call goes against you, appeal it, do not crowd the referee. You will see those other teams in the pit again.

Coopertition

Cooperation, then competition

Cooperation plus Competition. Take the points on the field, hand over what you know off it. Teams ask us about flywheel weights and how we stop jams, and we generally tell them. The V1.0 and V2.0 drawings are up for the same reason: they are retired anyway, so they might as well be useful.
